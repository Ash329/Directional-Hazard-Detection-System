How it works

Point your phone's camera ahead of you. Every second, the app checks the view for vehicles, people, bicycles, potholes and obstacles such as cones or barriers. It works out whether each one is on your left, ahead, or on your right, and how close it is, then speaks an alert when something needs your attention. When the path is clear, it stays quiet.
